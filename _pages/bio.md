- 2013 - 2016: Research and Teaching assistant at [Institute of Mathematics](https://www.wim.uni-mannheim.de/en/research/research-at-the-institute-of-mathematics/), University of Mannheim

- 2010: **Diploma** (Pure Mathematics; *with distinction*) at [Mathematisches Institut](https://www.uni-goettingen.de/en/20693.html), University of Göttingen
- Autumn 06/Winter 07: Exchange student at [Mathematics Institute](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/), Warwick University
