**Congratulations** to Jonas Brehmer on his successful PhD viva! Jonas' work on _Theory and Methodology of scoring functions: Tail properties, interval forecasts and point processes_ has led to several publications including _Why scoring rules cannot assess tail properties_ ([EJS 2019](https://doi.org/10.1214/19-EJS1622)), _Scoring interval forecasts: Equal-tailed, shortest, and model interval_ ([Bernoulli 2021](https://doi.org/10.3150/20-BEJ1298)) and _Comparative evaluation of point process forecasts_ ([AISM 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10463-023-00875-5)).
